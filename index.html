Jelly's Journey — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

EnglishEspañolFrançaisPortuguês

English

Jelly's Journey is a family adventure game. We built it so that it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data, and it does not share data with anyone.

Your game progress

Your progress and settings (stages played, memories found, language, sound volume) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Purchases

The first city is free. "The Full Journey" is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only checks, on your device, that the purchase was confirmed by the store.

The microphone (optional)

On Android phones, in one stage you may choose to blow into the phone to make wind. Only if you choose this, the game asks for microphone permission and reads how loud the sound is, on your device, while that stage is playing. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can always use the on-screen WIND button instead. On iPhone and iPad the game never uses the microphone.

Children

Jelly's Journey is made for all ages, including children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, no data about children is collected, used or shared. Purchases are behind a simple grown-ups check.

Internet

The game works offline. It only connects to the Google Play or Apple App Store service to complete or restore a purchase.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
